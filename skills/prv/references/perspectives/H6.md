# Method: reuse and known implementations on a plan
1. Each computation the design specifies (a query, an aggregation, a selection, a ranking, a search, a numeric routine) is named as its known problem type, with the formal definitions the requirement's words admit and the known algorithms for them and their complexity; a design that picks one of higher complexity than a known algorithm that meets the same requirement is a defect whatever the current input size, such as an all-pairs medoid where the member nearest the centroid takes one pass.
