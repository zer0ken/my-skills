# sql-expert sources

The skill follows the final ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI CS2023 Data Management knowledge units (D01), not the earlier SIGCSE 2022 draft. Rensselaer and Dartmouth academic reference notes supply standard relational modeling/theory (D02, D03, D28); the scholarly graph-query survey supplies graph semantics (D11); PostgreSQL and Neo4j manuals supply implementation-specific facts. The Kimball Group's published techniques, drawn from The Data Warehouse Toolkit, supply the dimensional-modeling subheadings and practice (D29-D38, D42-D43). Oracle's warehouse design manual corroborates normalized/ dimensional alternatives (D39). Bill Karwin's published SQL Antipatterns catalog and public Jaywalking excerpt supply named design antipatterns (D40-D41). Citation IDs in SKILL.md refer to the table below.

The section column identifies the material read, not just the sentence used to check access. A matched quotation establishes that text was fetched; it does not mechanically establish every claim's entailment. Product-specific guidance is conditional on the actual target product and version.

## Mechanical check

Every URL was fetched with `curl -sSL --compressed -m 20`. The full revision run verified 39 of 42 sources. D32 and D34 mismatches were ASCII-versus-ligature quotation transcription errors; their quotes were corrected against the fetched HTML, without fuzzy matching. D41 timed out with a partial PDF twice; curl's documented native `-C -` resume completed the first partial download in `citation-repair-2/`, with HTTP 206 and curl exit 0. The completed PDF was parsed by pdftotext and its literal quote checked. The resume rationale quotes curl's official manual: "Resume a previous transfer from the given byte offset." (https://curl.se/docs/manpage.html?category=23, --continue-at).

`citation-run-7/` consolidates the verified complete bodies, extracted text, and fetch records from those runs; it is not a claim of another full network fetch. `source_fetch_run` preserves provenance. Literal matching decodes HTML entities and normalizes whitespace; Unicode ligatures are retained. Timestamps, response-body SHA-256 hashes, curl exit codes, extraction methods, and per-source limits are in `evidence-table.json`. A successful HTTP 206 row is a completed resumed PDF, not a failed or silently truncated full response.

## Search path and exclusions

The search began with the database curriculum and textbook organization, then used functional dependency, lossless decomposition, dependency preservation, graph-pattern matching, navigational queries, and path semantics to refine the survey. Product-documentation searches supplied constraints, query semantics, plans, indexes, transactions, and logging. `search-log.json` records every search query and its batched-result provenance. The graph survey's sections 2, 3.1, 3.4, 4.1, and 4.5 were read beyond the abstract to distinguish matching from navigation and existence/endpoints from path enumeration. Its current-language claims were deliberately not carried forward as current product facts.

The revision added a search-discovered dimensional-modeling technique collection, a warehouse-design cross-check, and a published SQL antipattern catalog. The surveyed boundary has a field-defined taxonomy, standard relational theory, graph-query distinctions, warehouse schema design, and conditional product guidance. It is not an exhaustive literature survey. No target repository, organizational records, or actual running database were supplied for investigation; no claims about their behavior are made.

The following candidates support no claims in the final skill: two Database System Concepts slide PDFs exceeded curl's 20-second limit; BCcampus and its eCampusOntario mirror returned HTTP 403 to curl; the Ontario Tech candidate contained embedded text-generation instructions and was not used as an authority. Earlier downloaded bodies and survey manifests remain alongside the draft as access records. No quote from an inaccessible or excluded candidate is in the source table.

## Unsurveyed scope

Advanced recovery (including ARIES), buffer replacement, configuration-specific crash safety, distributed transactions and replication, security/privacy, vector/text/spatial/time-series access methods, data retention, provenance, governance, and legal/ethical obligations. Warehouse schema design is included; statistical and machine-learning analysis remain outside the assigned boundary. Advanced dimensional techniques (late-arriving data, bridges, SCD Types 4-7, allocations) and detailed EAV chapter tradeoffs remain unsurveyed. These headings remain visible in the skill; their presence does not assert expertise in the unsurveyed material.

The storage-contract paragraph and same-input EXCEPT ALL comparison are explicitly applications of the cited schema/query semantics, not claims that CS2023 prescribes an adapter-testing procedure. Per-input multiset equality does not establish equivalence for all legal inputs or engines.
